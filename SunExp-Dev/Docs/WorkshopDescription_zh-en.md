# SunExp / 日耀：烬冠天幕

## 中文

《日耀：烬冠天幕》是一个围绕 **日耀**、**炽灼天幕**、**聚炎** 与 **圣冕显化** 构筑的高风险爆发卡包与事件 Mod，新增 **30 张**可混入官方奖励池的卡牌、**13 件**日耀主题遗物，以及一个地图可见的 **日耀事件** 入口。三个卡包分支分别是【日耀：星火】、【日耀：烬冠】和【日耀：天幕】。

这套牌的核心思路是先把战场点燃，再把灼烧吸收为聚炎，最后用圣冕显化把爆发变成更安全的兑现窗口。它会主动制造全场灼烧，也会让玩家承受一定自燃压力；炽冕崩落可以在圣冕外强行引爆，但玩家也会承受相同伤害，因此需要用烬衣、遗物和圣冕高层阶段来控制风险。

整体强度偏向进攻和爆发，适合喜欢规划资源、承担代价并在关键回合打出高额输出的玩家。当前版本也加入了乌娜与白曜圣庭的阶段式事件记录；事件路线读完后会进入重复节点。数值与事件奖励仍可能根据反馈继续调整。

### 内容

- 卡包分支：【日耀：星火】、【日耀：烬冠】、【日耀：天幕】
- 30 张通用卡牌
- 13 件日耀主题遗物
- 1 个地图日耀事件入口，包含 6 段乌娜记录与重复节点
- 核心机制：日耀、聚炎、炽灼天幕、灼烧
- 爆发窗口：圣冕显化、炽冕崩落
- C# DLL、CSV、文本与图片资源

### 使用方式

在游戏的卡包选择界面启用 **日耀：烬冠天幕** 的相关分支后，这 30 张卡会像官方卡包一样进入奖励 / 获取池。遗物也归属于这些卡包分支，可以在卡包详情中查看。地图上的 **日耀事件** 会逐层推进乌娜与白曜圣庭的记录，当前路线读完后会进入重复事件。



## English

## SunExp / Solar Charge

SunExp is a high-risk burst card pack, partner, and event mod built around **Solar Radiance**, **Scorching Canopy**, **Gathered Flame**, **Ember**, and **Crown Manifestation**. It adds **30 cards** that can be mixed into the official reward pool, **13 Solar relics**, the **Dusk** partner, and a map-visible **Solar Event** route across the Radiance: Spark, Radiance: Ember Crown, and Radiance: Canopy branches.

The core loop is to ignite the battlefield, absorb Burn into Gathered Flame, then use Crown Manifestation as the safer window for cashing that stored heat out. Blazing Crown Collapse can still be forced outside Crown Manifestation, but you take the same burst damage as the enemies, so managing self-burn through Ember Cloak, relics, and high Crown phases is part of the intended play pattern.

Its power level leans toward offense and burst. It is designed for players who enjoy planning resources, accepting risk, and setting up decisive high-damage turns. The current version also adds staged Una / Obsidian Sanctuary records through the Solar Event route; after the current route is complete, it falls back to a repeat node. Balance and event rewards may change based on feedback.

### Contents

- Pack branches: Radiance: Spark, Radiance: Ember Crown, Radiance: Canopy
- 30 general-purpose cards
- 13 Solar relics
- 1 Solar map event route with 6 Una records and a repeat node
- Core mechanics: Solar Radiance, Gathered Flame, Scorching Canopy, Burn
- Burst window: Crown Manifestation, Blazing Crown Collapse
- C# DLL, CSV, text, and image resources

### How to Use

Enable the **SunExp / 日耀：烬冠天幕** branches in the in-game card pack selection screen. The 30 cards will then enter the reward and acquisition pool like official card packs. The relics also belong to these pack branches and can be viewed in the pack details. The **Solar Event** appears on the map and advances Una's Obsidian Sanctuary records, then falls back to a repeat event after the current route is complete.
