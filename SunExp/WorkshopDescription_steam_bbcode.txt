[h1]SunExp / 日耀聚能[/h1]

《日耀聚能》是一个围绕 [b]日耀[/b]、[b]聚炎[/b] 与 [b]圣冕显化[/b] 构筑的高风险爆发卡包，新增 [b]30 张[/b]可混入官方奖励池的卡牌，以及 [b]13 件[/b]日耀主题遗物，并分为基础、聚爆、天幕三个卡包分支。

这套牌的核心思路是先把战场点燃，再把灼烧吸收为聚炎，最后用圣冕显化把爆发变成更安全的兑现窗口。它会主动制造全场灼烧，也会让玩家承受一定自燃压力；冕核爆闪可以在圣冕外强行引爆，但玩家也会承受相同伤害，因此需要用避灼光幕、遗物和圣冕高层阶段来控制风险。

整体强度偏向进攻和爆发，适合喜欢规划资源、承担代价并在关键回合打出高额输出的玩家。当前版本仍在继续打磨，数值可能会根据反馈继续调整。

[h2]内容[/h2]

[list]
[*]卡包分支：基础包、聚爆包、天幕包
[*]30 张通用卡牌
[*]13 件日耀主题遗物
[*]核心机制：日耀、聚炎、日耀场、灼烧
[*]爆发窗口：圣冕显化、冕核爆闪
[*]纯数据 Mod，不包含 DLL Hook
[/list]

[h2]使用方式[/h2]

在游戏的卡包选择界面启用 [b]日耀聚能[/b] 的相关分支后，这 30 张卡会像官方卡包一样进入奖励 / 获取池。遗物也归属于这些卡包分支，可以在卡包详情中查看。

[h1]English[/h1]

[h2]SunExp / Solar Charge[/h2]

SunExp is a high-risk burst card pack built around [b]Solar Radiance[/b], [b]Gathered Flame[/b], and [b]Crown Manifestation[/b]. It adds [b]30 cards[/b] that can be mixed into the official reward pool, along with [b]13 Solar relics[/b], across Base, Burst, and Canopy pack branches.

The core loop is to ignite the battlefield, absorb Burn into Gathered Flame, then use Crown Manifestation as the safer window for cashing that stored heat out. Crown-Core Flash can still be forced outside Crown Manifestation, but you take the same burst damage as the enemies, so managing self-burn through Burn Ward, relics, and high Crown phases is part of the intended play pattern.

Its power level leans toward offense and burst. It is designed for players who enjoy planning resources, accepting risk, and setting up decisive high-damage turns. The current version is still being polished, and balance may change based on feedback.

[h2]Contents[/h2]

[list]
[*]Pack branches: Base, Burst, Canopy
[*]30 general-purpose cards
[*]13 Solar relics
[*]Core mechanics: Solar Radiance, Gathered Flame, Solar Field, Burn
[*]Burst window: Crown Manifestation, Crown-Core Flash
[*]Pure data mod, no DLL hook included
[/list]

[h2]How to Use[/h2]

Enable the [b]SunExp / 日耀聚能[/b] branches in the in-game card pack selection screen. The 30 cards will then enter the reward and acquisition pool like official card packs. The relics also belong to these pack branches and can be viewed in the pack details.
