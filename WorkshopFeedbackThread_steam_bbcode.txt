[h1]SunExp / 日耀聚能 - 反馈与建议收集[/h1]

感谢游玩《日耀聚能》。

作者：Aura

这个卡包目前仍在继续打磨，我希望它能做到：

[list]
[*]能和官方卡包正常混玩
[*]日耀、聚炎和灼烧循环清晰易懂
[*]爆发上限足够有爽感，但需要承担自燃与节奏代价
[*]遗物能帮助体系成型，而不是单独决定胜负
[*]卡面和遗物图标尽量接近原版风格
[/list]

[h2]希望收集的反馈[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]数值平衡[/b]：哪些卡牌或遗物太强、太弱、费用不合理，或者爆发过高。
[*][b]自燃压力[/b]：聚炎、日耀场和圣冕显化带来的自身灼烧是否过重或过轻。
[*][b]出现率[/b]：是否感觉 Mod 卡或遗物出现太频繁，或者很难遇到。
[*][b]构筑体验[/b]：日耀、聚炎、灼烧吸收和圣冕爆发是否顺手，有没有卡住节奏。
[*][b]Bug / 异常[/b]：卡牌不生效、文本错误、图片丢失、卡包无法启用、战斗中报错等。
[*][b]美术表现[/b]：卡面和遗物图标是否像原版，哪些图需要重画或调整。
[*][b]兼容性[/b]：和其他 Mod 一起使用时是否出现问题。
[/list]

[h1]SunExp - Feedback & Suggestions[/h1]

Thank you for playing SunExp.

Created by Aura.

This card pack is still being polished. The current goal is:

[list]
[*]Playable alongside official card packs
[*]A clear Solar Radiance, Gathered Flame, and Burn loop
[*]High burst potential that still asks the player to manage self-burn and tempo costs
[*]Relics that help the engine come together without deciding the run by themselves
[*]Card and relic art that feels close to the original style
[/list]

[h2]Feedback Wanted[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Balance[/b]: cards or relics that feel too strong, too weak, too expensive, or too explosive.
[*][b]Self-burn pressure[/b]: whether Gathered Flame, Solar Field, and Crown Manifestation create too much or too little self-burn.
[*][b]Appearance rate[/b]: whether mod cards or relics appear too often or too rarely.
[*][b]Build feel[/b]: whether Solar Radiance, Gathered Flame, Burn absorption, and Crown burst feel smooth to play.
[*][b]Bugs / issues[/b]: cards not working, text errors, missing art, pack selection issues, combat errors, etc.
[*][b]Art style[/b]: whether card and relic art feels close enough to the original style.
[*][b]Compatibility[/b]: issues when used together with other mods.
[/list]
